EXTRAFrases sociales (Practical English)

Reaccionar a noticias, dar opiniones, expresiones fijas. No está en la lista de tu profesora, pero es del libro — útil para Writing y Speaking.

Nota: este material es de menor prioridad que los Días 1-7. Revísalo si te sobra tiempo.

1. Reaccionar a lo que dicen (Episode 1, p.10)

Lista: How fantastic! · That's great news! · I don't believe it! · You're kidding. · What a pity. · Never mind. · Oh, no! · Really?

Ejercicio A — Escribe una reacción válida de la lista

  1. A: "Kate's going to study abroad for a year!" B: ______ →
  2. A: "I left my wallet at home again!" B: ______ →
  3. A: "I didn't get the job." B: ______ →
  4. A: "We're getting married!" B: ______ →
  5. A: "I lost my phone." B: ______ →

How + adjetivo, What + sustantivo

How + adjetivo (sin sustantivo): How interesting! · What (a/an) + sustantivo: What a good idea!

  1. interesting!
  2. a good idea!
  3. terrible news!
  4. awful!
  5. amazing!
  6. a pity!

Social English

Lista: a really nice guy · Go ahead · How do you see · How incredible · I mean · Not really · That's because · things like that

  1. A: What do you think of Isabel's new boyfriend? B: He's ______. →
  2. A: ______ your life in ten years? B: I think I'll be married and have my own company. →
  3. A: I hear you're an excellent swimmer. Would you like to be a professional? B: ______. I don't have enough time to train. →
  4. A: I'm sorry, I'm not feeling hungry. B: ______ you ate too much for lunch! →
  5. A: You know, I think we went to the same school. B: ______! What a coincidence! →
  6. A: Can I have another piece of chicken, please? B: ______. There's more in the kitchen. →
  7. A: What kinds of books do you read? B: Biographies, historical fiction, ______. →
  8. A: Would you like to come to the concert with us? B: No, sorry. ______, I'd love to, but I'm busy. →

2. Dar opiniones (Episode 2, p.24)

Frases para dar opinión: in my opinion · personally, I think · to be honest · if you ask me · don't you agree?

Ejercicio D — Completa con la frase de opinión correcta

  1. A: What do you think of music festivals? B: They're OK. ______, there are too many people. →
  2. A: Do you think Cathy has a good voice? B: ______, I've never heard her sing. →
  3. A: Do you ever listen to the radio? B: Not really. The DJs talk too much. ______? →
  4. A: For me, the 80s was the best decade for music. B: ______, the 80s were good, but there's been great music since then too. →

Social English

Lista: Hang on a minute · It's just that my boyfriend's away · You've come back · Did you mean what you said

  1. A: ______ B: Yes, I forgot my phone. →
  2. A: ______ about moving abroad? B: No, of course I didn't. I was only kidding. →
  3. A: You look upset. What's the matter? B: Nothing really. ______ and I miss him. →
  4. A: I'm going out for a walk. Do you want to come? B: ______. I'll get my coat. →
