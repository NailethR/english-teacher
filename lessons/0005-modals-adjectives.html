DÍA 5 / 7Modales + Reflexivos + Adjetivos -ed/-ing

should · must · have to · can · could · be able to — y cómo describir emociones correctamente.

Meta de hoy: resolver sin dudar los ejercicios de obligación/prohibición y habilidad del examen, y nunca más confundir "I'm bored" con "I'm boring".

1. Obligación y prohibición

Repasa la tabla de referencia antes de empezar. Recuerda: must not ≠ don't have to.

Ejercicio A — must, must not, should o shouldn't

Lista: must · must not · should · shouldn't

  1. Adam graduate from high school or he won't be able to go to college.
  2. I think you buy the blue dress, not the red one. It suits you better.
  3. You take phones into the exam room.
  4. You eat so much junk food. It isn't good for you.
  5. You be quiet when you're in the library.
  6. I know I go out tonight, but I really want to go to the concert.

Ejercicio B — have to / don't have to / must not (obligación externa vs. prohibición)

Lista: have to · don't have to · must not

  1. You have a visa to enter the US. (es un requisito legal externo)
  2. You pay to visit most museums. Entrance is usually free.
  3. You smoke in any public building. It is prohibited by law.
  4. You go everywhere by subway. You can take the bus.

2. Habilidad: can / could / was able to

Ejercicio C — Elige la forma correcta (dos opciones, como en el libro)

1. She ______ swim really well because she used to live by the ocean.

2. You need to ______ drive to live in the country.

3. Luke ______ read when he was only three years old.

4. We're really sorry we ______ come to your wedding.

5. I used to ______ speak a little Arabic, but I've forgotten most of it now.

3. Pronombres reflexivos

Ejercicio D — Completa con el pronombre reflexivo

Lista: myself · yourself · himself · herself · itself · ourselves · yourselves · themselves

  1. He fell down the stairs, but luckily he didn't hurt .
  2. Have a great time, you two! I'm sure you'll enjoy .
  3. We really wanted to watch the sunrise, so we kept awake by listening to music.
  4. My new printer turns off when it isn't being used.

4. Adjetivos -ed / -ing

Ejercicio E — -ed o -ing (dos opciones, como en el libro)

1. Venice is an ______ city. You really must go there. (amazed/amazing)

2. I felt very ______ when I realized my mistake. (embarrassed/embarrassing)

3. He's ______ of dogs. (frightened/frightening)

4. I enjoyed the book, but the movie was a little ______. (bored/boring)

5. Max was very ______ when he wasn't chosen for the job. (disappointed/disappointing)

6. I got very ______ when I couldn't log onto my bank's website. (frustrated/frustrating)

💬 Si -ed/-ing o must/have to te siguen costando después de este ejercicio, dime específicamente cuál oración y seguimos ahí antes del Día 6.
