DÍA 3 / 7Present Perfect Continuous + Past Perfect

for/since, acciones que siguen, y "el pasado del pasado".

Meta de hoy: usar for/since correctamente, dominar Present Perfect Continuous, y entender cuándo el Past Perfect es obligatorio (no opcional) en una historia.

1. for vs. since

for + duración (cuánto tiempo): for a month, for six years, for a long time.
since + punto de inicio (desde cuándo): since 2015, since Tuesday, since I was young.

Ejercicio A — for o since

  1. I've had my car about a month.
  2. My mom has been sick weeks.
  3. We've known each other we were in college.
  4. Owen has worked for the same company five years.

2. Present Perfect Continuous

have/has been + V-ing — la acción empezó en el pasado y sigue ahora (o acaba de parar y ves su efecto).

Ejercicio B — Completa con Present Perfect Continuous

  1. A: Did you know Amy's in a band? B: No. together for a long time?
  2. A: How long was your flight? B: Twelve hours. We all day.
  3. A: Why does Marco's teacher want to see you? B: his homework lately.
  4. A: You're late. B: Sorry. long?
  5. A: You look exhausted. B: Yes, the kids all day!

3. Past Perfect — el pasado del pasado

Cuando cuentas una historia en pasado y necesitas hablar de algo que pasó ANTES de ese momento pasado, usas had + V3.
