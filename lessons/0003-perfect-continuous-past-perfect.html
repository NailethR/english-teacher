DÍA 3 / 7Present Perfect Continuous + Past Perfect

for/since, acciones que siguen, y "el pasado del pasado".

Meta de hoy: usar for/since correctamente, dominar Present Perfect Continuous, y entender cuándo el Past Perfect es obligatorio (no opcional) en una historia.

1. for vs. since

for + duración (cuánto tiempo): for a month, for six years, for a long time.
since + punto de inicio (desde cuándo): since 2015, since Tuesday, since I was young.

2. Present Perfect Continuous

have/has been + V-ing — la acción empezó en el pasado y sigue ahora (o acaba de parar y ves su efecto).

Ejercicio B — Completa con Present Perfect Continuous

  1. A: Did you know Amy's in a band? B: No. ______ (they/play) together for a long time? →
  2. A: How long was your flight? B: Twelve hours. We ______ (travel) all day. →
  3. A: My brother has a very good job in New York. B: Really? How long ______ (he/work) there? →
  4. A: Diana has finally moved into a new apartment. B: Oh good! ______ (she/look for) one for a long time. →
  5. A: Why does Marco's teacher want to see you? B: ______ (he/not/do) his homework lately. →
  6. A: You're late. B: Sorry. ______ (you/wait) long? →
  7. A: You look exhausted. B: Yes, ______ (I/look after) the kids all day! →
  8. A: How's your soccer team doing, Pete? B: Pretty good, but ______ (I/not/play) recently. I hurt my foot. →

3. Past Perfect — el pasado del pasado

Cuando cuentas una historia en pasado y necesitas hablar de algo que pasó ANTES de ese momento pasado, usas had + V3.

Ejercicio C — Simple Past, Past Continuous o Past Perfect

  1. She ______ (win) the gold medal at the last Olympic Games. →
  2. I wanted to swim, but I ______ (not/bring) a towel. (ya era tarde para conseguirlo) →
  3. Our guests arrived while we ______ (watch) the basketball game. →
  4. As soon as the movie started, I realized that I ______ (see) it before. →
  5. I ______ (drive) when you called, so I couldn't answer. →
  6. When we arrived, everyone else ______ (finish) their lunch and they ______ (sit) on the patio. → /
💬 El Past Perfect suele ser el más difícil de los tres. Si te cuesta decidir el orden de las tres acciones en una historia, pregúntame y lo resolvemos con una línea de tiempo.
