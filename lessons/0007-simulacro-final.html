READING — practica el formato True/False/Doesn't say

Carla has always been very competitive. She used to play soccer every weekend when she was in high school, but she stopped when she started college. Last year, she joined a running club because she wanted to make new friends. She has been training with them for eight months now, and last month she ran her first 10K race. She was really excited about it, even though she didn't win. Her best friend Diego, who she met at the running club, has become like family to her. They usually go for coffee together after every training session.

12. True, False o Doesn't say (escribe: True / False / Doesn't say)

  1. Carla still plays soccer every weekend. →
  2. Carla joined the running club to get in shape. →
  3. Carla has been training with the running club for less than a year. →
  4. Carla won her first 10K race. →
  5. Diego and Carla met through the running club. →

Marco has never been very good with money. He used to spend everything he earned on clothes and eating out, and he never saved anything. Two years ago, he inherited some money from his grandfather, and for the first time, he decided to invest it instead of wasting it. He's been working with a financial advisor since then, and he has already paid back the loan he took out for his car. He still owes money on his mortgage, but he's saving more than he used to, and he's fed up with living paycheck to paycheck.

  2. Marco inherited money from his grandmother. →

  4. Marco has a financial advisor. →

WRITING — practica libre

Elige uno de los dos temas (140-180 palabras):

Opción A: Describe a una persona de tu familia o un amigo cercano. Responde: ¿Cómo es su personalidad? ¿Qué le gusta o no le gusta hacer? ¿Qué está haciendo ahora mismo en su vida (Present Continuous/Present Perfect)? ¿Cómo se llevan ustedes (get along)?

Opción B: Cuenta la historia de una amistad o relación de tu infancia. ¿Cuándo se conocieron? ¿Qué solían hacer juntos (used to)? ¿Siguen en contacto?
